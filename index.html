      How it works

      Shared principles

      • Us is everyone credited on the production, guests included.
      • Generated by AI counts the same as bought, downloaded, ripped or licensed: not ours.
      • Most or all of a category not being ours is much worse than some of it.
      • The framework comes first and is shown on the label (“Built with TiXL”). A third-party engine or demotool, or a mostly generated framework, means B at best — unless you are one of the authors of that tool (“Built with TiXL — our own tool”).
      • Labelling is voluntary. A label is a public claim; if it turns out to be false, organizers may disqualify the entry. You may re-issue a corrected label any time before someone else raises the issue.
      Prototype. The scoring is opinionated on purpose — the answers behind each label are always one click away.
